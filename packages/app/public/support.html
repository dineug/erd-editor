Support

erd-editor.io is a free, open-source editor for Entity-Relationship Diagrams, run by dineug. For help with the editor, including its Google Drive editor at erd-editor.io/gdrive, email support@erd-editor.io.

What to include

  • What you were doing, and what happened instead.
  • The page you were on: erd-editor.io, or erd-editor.io/gdrive for the Google Drive editor.
  • Your browser and operating system.
  • Any message ERD Editor showed you.

Never send a password. Leave out the content of a diagram you would rather keep private; a small example that shows the problem is enough.

Guides

The Editing Guide explains how to use the editor.

Google Drive access

To end ERD Editor's access to your Google Drive, sign out of ERD Editor at erd-editor.io/gdrive, or remove ERD Editor from the apps linked to your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections. Your files stay in your Google Drive either way.

Bug reports and feature requests

You can also open an issue at github.com/dineug/erd-editor/issues. Issues are public, so leave personal details out of them.
